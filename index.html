I · Wordmark

The Primary Lockup

The Watchhouse Advisory wordmark is two lines, locked to a shared right edge. "Watchhouse" sits in Cormorant Garamond at 400 weight. "Advisory" sits below in the same family at 300 weight, all caps, with letter-spacing at 0.32em. Both lines terminate at the same right margin. A 0.5px brass rule fills the variable space between the start of the W and the start of "Advisory," anchoring the suffix to the wordmark above.

Usage Rules
  • ·The wordmark may never be set without "Advisory" attached. The two are inseparable.
  • ·Both lines share a fixed right margin. "Advisory" never floats away from this edge.
  • ·The brass rule may not be replaced with another color, removed, or rendered as a solid block.
  • ·Minimum size is 22pt for "Watchhouse." Below this, the suffix becomes illegible.
  • ·The wordmark may appear in deep forest green on cream/white, or cream on deep forest green. No other color combinations are permitted.
II · Mark

The W in Double Frame

The mark is a singular Cormorant Garamond capital W set in a double-frame escutcheon. The outer frame is deep forest green; the inner frame is antique brass. The W is set at 700 weight — the heaviest cut available in the Cormorant family — to function as a mark rather than as letterform.

The mark may stand independently of the wordmark. Used as a favicon, a report-cover seal, an embossed letterhead element, or wherever space is too constrained for the full lockup.

Usage Notes

Forest green carries every primary application — wordmark, headers, report covers, button states. Brass appears sparingly as horizontal rules, accent strokes, and key emphasis. It is structural, not decorative. Cream is the paper of the brand — appearing as background on report covers, marketing materials, and the website hero. The three risk colors appear only inside reports as visual indicators on risk-rating tables. They never appear in the marketing system, on the website, or in correspondence.

IV · Typography

Three Roles, Two Serifs

The system uses two serifs (Cormorant Garamond for display, EB Garamond for body) and one sans-serif (Inter for accents and data). The combination produces the strongest old-establishment signal while remaining fully readable in long-form analytical writing.

X · Summary

What This Adds Up To

A firm that could have been founded in 1923, operating in 2026 with modern tools. Restrained, scholarly, serious without solemnity. Built around a single discipline. The visual world reinforces the institutional voice: nothing is hurried, nothing is loud, and every element does work.

Visual Direction · V1 · Internal
Brand Principles
·Restraint signals confidence. The firms that need to over-design are the firms without institutional weight. Watchhouse Advisory's substance comes from operating as if it doesn't need to perform anything.
·The right edge is sacred. The wordmark's right margin is the brand's structural commitment. It is never broken.
·The brass rule is structural, not decorative. Every appearance of brass does work — separating, anchoring, measuring. It is the brand's punctuation.
·Serif typography carries the institutional voice. No sans-serif appears in display contexts. The brand reads the way old academic publishing reads.
·The firm operates behind the work. No founders pictured. No personalities promoted. The work product is the brand's voice.
